## ⚡ What Is This Protocol?

The **Fibonacci Confluence Zone Rulebook** is a zero-ambiguity trading operating system. It translates crowd psychology, market geometry, and Fibonacci expansion/contraction cycles into **217 deterministic, machine-parsable trading laws**.

### ❌ What This Is NOT:
* ❌ Drawing arbitrary lines across random highs and lows
* ❌ Guessing support or front-running unconfirmed reversals
* ❌ Subjective "looks like a bounce" discretion

### ✅ What This IS:
* 🎯 **3+ Level Clustering:** Only zones where at least three independent Fibonacci measurements converge within a tight band (≤ 10% of asset price) are valid.
* 🎯 **The Confirmation Law:** Price touching a zone is *never* an entry signal. A mechanical trigger (lower-timeframe pattern or prior bar high/low breach) is strictly required.
* 🎯 **Mathematical Symmetry:** Time and price swing symmetry matching ($A:B = C:D$) to catch turning points before retail orders flood the book.
* 🎯 **Surgical Risk Math:** Fixed 2%–3% capital allocation formulas, trailing-stop mechanics, and mandatory 50% scale-out targets.

## 🔬 The 4 Core Price Studies

Every Confluence Zone is forged by combining these four distinct studies:

| Study | Formula / Anchor | Primary Ratios | Role |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Retracements** | Countertrend move of prior swing $X:A$ ($< 100\%$) | `0.382`, `0.500`, `0.618`, `0.786` | Locates primary pullback depth |
| **2. Extensions** | Moves penetrating beyond prior anchor $X$ ($> 100\%$) | `1.272`, `1.618` (and `2.618`) | Exhaustion points & trap setups |
| **3. Projections** | Prior swing $X:A$ applied from opposite pivot point $B$ | `0.618`, `1.000` (Symmetry), `1.618` | Measures balanced swing legs ($AB=CD$) |
| **4. Expansions** | Range $X:A$ extended outward directly from point $A$ | `0.618`, `1.000`, `1.618` | Confirmatory filter study only |
